Floor Panel turns your floor plan into a live Home Assistant wall panel. Your rooms glow with their temperature, lights and heating show where they're on, open windows swing open on the plan, and a tap on a room opens its controls.

This starter kit gives you the panel plus a simple plan editor: open a picture of your floor plan in your browser, click the corners of each room, name them, mark doors and windows, and save. No drawing software, no YAML.

What it shows

  • Every room on your plan, coloured by temperature, with humidity, presence and motion
  • Lights and heating glowing where they're on; tap to switch, or open the room for dimming and radiator controls
  • Open windows drawn open on the plan
  • Several floors, one at a time, with a floor switcher
  • Weather, sun, bin collection days, alarm state and a doorbell camera pop-up
  • A bedtime check: open windows, lights still on, tomorrow's bins
  • Light by day, dark at night

What's included

  • The panel (one HTML page plus a tiny Home Assistant connection script)
  • editor.html: draw your own home over a picture of your plan
  • A fictional demo house (a two-storey semi), so it works as soon as you copy it in
  • config.example.json: point each room at your sensors and lights in one file
  • A demo mode with made-up states, and a setup view that numbers every window
  • A step-by-step guide

Requirements

  • Home Assistant 2024.1 or newer on your home network
  • A wall screen whose browser supports ES2017 (roughly 2017 or later): an old Android tablet, a Fire tablet or a spare phone. Wall-mounted displays such as the Shelly Wall Display should work but haven't been tested
  • A way to copy files into Home Assistant's config/www folder (File editor, Studio Code Server or Samba add-on)
  • A computer to draw your plan (the editor works best with a mouse)

Good to know

  • It uses Home Assistant's normal login. No token or password is stored in any file.
  • Your plan picture stays in your browser: it is never uploaded or saved in the panel files.
  • Files in Home Assistant's www folder can be read by anyone on your home network without logging in, so your room names and entity ids are visible on your network (states and controls are not).
  • Rather not draw it yourself? The made-for-you Floor Plan Panel service traces your plan for you.
  • Made with AI assistance: the panel grew out of one running on a real wall display, then was generalised with an AI tool. Tested against a live Home Assistant 2026.9 with its built-in demo devices (sign-in, live temperatures and motion, switching lights, staying signed in after a reload), and in demo mode at square, landscape and portrait sizes. Not yet tried on every wall tablet, so please tell us if something doesn't work on your setup.
  • Thermostats running in a heat/cool range mode show their modes but no single target temperature. The screenshots are the fictional demo house, not a real home.
