How it works

1. Buy this listing.

2. Email a photo, scan or PDF of your floor plan to williams24.ha+plans@gmail.com with your order number. Up to 3 images, for example one per floor.

3. Within 2 days you get a watermarked proof by email. Reply "yes" to approve it, or tell us what to change: room names, a missing room, anything that's off. Two rounds of changes are included.

4. Once you approve, the final files arrive by email.

What you get

  • A personalised A3 print-ready PDF and high-resolution PNG
  • An A6 folded-card PDF and PNG with a small plan on the front and your message inside
  • A short guide: How to give it.txt
  • Minimal, blueprint or warm style; one seasonal theme is included if you name it

Ideal for a new home, a housewarming or an estate-agent closing gift. Nothing is shipped; print the files locally.
