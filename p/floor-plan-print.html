Send us a photo or scan of your home's floor plan and we turn it into a clean, modern A3 print to frame: a lovely housewarming or new-home gift.

How it works

1. Buy this listing.

2. Email a photo, scan or PDF of your floor plan to williams24.ha+plans@gmail.com with your order number. Up to 3 images, for example one per floor.

3. Within 2 days you get a watermarked proof by email. Reply "yes" to approve it, or tell us what to change: room names, a missing room, anything that's off. Two rounds of changes are included.

4. Once you approve, the final files arrive by email.

What you get

  • An A3 print-ready vector PDF and a high-resolution PNG (about 300 dpi)
  • Your choice of style: minimal, blueprint or warm. Say which in your email; we use minimal if you don't.
  • Room names as on your plan; several floors side by side, labelled

Good to know

  • This is a digital file to print at home or at any print shop. Nothing is shipped.
  • Your images and plan are deleted 30 days after delivery. Reply "keep my plan" to keep it for 12 months (handy for a seasonal theme later; buying a theme keeps it automatically).
  • Plans are traced with AI assistance and every proof is checked by a person before it reaches you.
  • The pictures show made-up sample homes, not a customer's home.

Questions? Reply to your receipt.
