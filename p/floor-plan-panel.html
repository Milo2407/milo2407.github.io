Your own home as a live Home Assistant wall panel. Send us your floor plan and get a fast panel that shows your real rooms: each one coloured by its temperature, lights glowing when they're on, open windows lighting up, heating showing where it runs. Tap a room for its lights, radiator and blinds.

How it works

1. Buy this listing.

2. Email a photo, scan or PDF of your floor plan to williams24.ha+plans@gmail.com with your order number. Up to 3 images, for example one per floor.

3. Within 2 days you get a watermarked proof by email. Reply "yes" to approve it, or tell us what to change: room names, a missing room, anything that's off. Two rounds of changes are included.

4. Once you approve, the final files arrive by email.

What you get

  • A ready-made panel drawn from your plan: one small HTML page (about 100 KB), with day and night looks and a floor switcher for homes with several floors
  • config.json, where you link each room to your Home Assistant entities. The guide walks you through it, and a setup view numbers every window so window sensors are easy to match.
  • A step-by-step install guide (copy a folder into Home Assistant's www folder, open one address on your screen)
  • Personal-use licence

Requirements

  • Home Assistant 2024.1 or newer on your home network
  • A screen with a browser: Shelly Wall Display, a tablet, a Fire tablet or a phone
  • A way to copy files into Home Assistant (File editor, Studio Code Server or Samba add-on)

Good to know

  • No HACS or custom cards needed; nothing to install in Home Assistant itself.
  • Bathrooms are drawn but stay plain, for privacy.
  • Tested in demo mode in a desktop browser at square, landscape and portrait sizes; not tried on every tablet model.
  • Independent product, not affiliated with Home Assistant or Shelly.
  • Your images and plan are deleted 30 days after delivery. Reply "keep my plan" to keep it for 12 months (handy for a seasonal theme later; buying a theme keeps it automatically).
  • Plans are traced with AI assistance and every proof is checked by a person before it reaches you.
  • The pictures show made-up sample homes, not a customer's home.

Questions? Reply to your receipt.
