Brightglen privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026.

This website

This website is static. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics and collects no personal data. The live demo runs entirely in your browser with made-up data and never connects to a Home Assistant.

Our Pinterest tool

Brightglen uses a small private tool, connected to the Pinterest API, to publish Pins about our own products to our own Pinterest account (pinterest.com/brightglen). It reads only our own boards and Pins. It does not access, collect or store data about other Pinterest users, and it is not offered to anyone else.

Orders

When you buy from Brightglen on Etsy or Gumroad, those platforms handle payment and their privacy policies apply. Floor plans and images you send us for a custom order are used only to make your order and are deleted 30 days after delivery unless you ask us to keep them.

Contact

Questions about privacy: reply to your order receipt, or message Brightglen on Etsy or Gumroad.
